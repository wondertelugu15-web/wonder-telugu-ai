# Wonder Telugu Health Tips — Episode 001
## Topic
చర్మం పొడిబారినప్పుడు సాధారణ సంరక్షణ

## Format
- Vertical 9:16
- Target: approximately 45–60 seconds
- Visual style: cute expressive 3D-inspired characters on macro skin-like backgrounds, playful educational tone, Telugu voice-over and captions.
- This pilot uses original illustrated characters; it is not a medical diagnosis or treatment claim.

## Safety notes
- General educational information only; not a diagnosis, cure, or personalized medical advice.
- Do not claim shea butter cures eczema, infection, wounds, or any disease.
- Include patch-test and stop-use advice for irritation.
- Encourage medical advice for persistent, painful, cracked, infected, or worsening skin symptoms.

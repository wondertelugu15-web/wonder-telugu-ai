## Scene-by-scene video prompts

### Scene 1: Kitchen disagreement
Wide and medium shots in a clean traditional Telugu village kitchen. Mother-in-law turns toward younger daughter-in-law and speaks with a stern but believable expression. Younger daughter-in-law pauses her work, looks up and answers respectfully. Older daughter-in-law is visible listening in the background and reacts with concern. Accurate Telugu lip-sync for each speaker.

### Scene 2: Sister-in-law supports her
Older daughter-in-law steps closer and gently supports the younger one. Mother-in-law turns to her with a surprised, mildly irritated look. Younger daughter-in-law resumes preparing the food. Distinct speaker turns and natural eye-lines.

### Scene 3: Brothers enter
Both brothers enter the kitchen courtyard from the doorway. They look between the women and respond with light humour. Mother-in-law folds her arms; younger daughter-in-law smiles briefly. Keep each brother visually distinct.

### Scene 4: Food reveal
Close-ups of hands plating three original leftover-rice dishes, then a medium shot as the younger daughter-in-law presents them. Father-in-law approaches, smells the food and invites everyone to taste. Preserve hand anatomy and food continuity.

### Scene 5: Reconciliation
Mother-in-law tastes the food, pauses, then smiles warmly and praises the younger daughter-in-law. Older daughter-in-law smiles; brothers react naturally. Use clear speaker-specific mouth movement and visible listening reactions.

### Scene 6: Family ending
The family gathers around the dining area. Mother-in-law invites the younger daughter-in-law to sit beside her. The younger daughter-in-law gives a natural subscribe/share line to camera. End with everyone smiling and sharing the meal.

## Mandatory quality gate
Do not publish if any character's face changes between shots, the wrong person moves their lips, Telugu speech is mispronounced, hands/faces are malformed, or actions do not match dialogue. A static image montage or generic cooking footage is not an acceptable substitute.

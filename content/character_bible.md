# Wonder Telugu — Permanent Character Bible

Visual target: photorealistic Telugu/Andhra joint-family drama, based on the two user-provided sample videos. Use them as style references only; create original characters, dialogue, scenes and footage.

## Identity lock (must remain unchanged across episodes)
- అత్తయ్య: woman, 48–55; mature Telugu village-mother appearance; neat traditional cotton/silk sarees; expressive but natural face; mature female voice.
- పెద్ద కోడలు: woman, 28–33; neat traditional saree; confident, warm expression; distinct young-adult female voice.
- చిన్న కోడలు: woman, 25–30; neat traditional saree; lively but grounded expression; a different face and a different young-adult female voice from the elder daughter-in-law.
- పెద్ద కొడుకు: man, 32–35; clean traditional shirt and dhoti; distinct adult male voice.
- చిన్న కొడుకు: man, 29–32; different face and styling from elder brother; distinct adult male voice.
- మామయ్య: man, 55–60; dignified village elder; neat shirt and dhoti; mature, calm male voice.
- Children: age-appropriate, modest traditional clothing, natural child voices only where safe and supported.

## Visual and acting rules
1. Lock each character's face reference, hair, age, complexion, clothing palette and voice identity. Never randomly redesign a returning character.
2. Photorealistic human appearance, clear eyes, hands and facial anatomy; clean, well-lit village home/kitchen/courtyard.
3. Telugu dialogue must match the visible speaker's mouth movement. Only the active speaker should deliver the line; listeners must show context-appropriate reactions.
4. Include real actions motivated by dialogue: chopping, stirring, serving, looking, turning, walking, smiling, pausing and responding. No frozen slideshow, generic looped cooking footage or unrelated B-roll.
5. Keep continuity across shots: props, food, sarees, jewellery, room layout and screen direction.
6. Begin with a family disagreement; develop a clear story; include a natural like/share/subscribe request by a character; end with reconciliation and affection.
7. Do not publish automatically until face consistency, voice separation, Telugu pronunciation, lip-sync and action/reaction quality pass human review.

## Honest production constraint
The current repository has a basic voice-over and clip-concatenation pipeline. It does not yet implement multi-character talking-human video generation or verified Telugu lip-sync. Do not describe the current pipeline as satisfying those requirements. Do not call paid APIs unless the owner explicitly approves the cost.

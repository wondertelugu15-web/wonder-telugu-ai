## Visual and acting rules
1. Lock face reference, hair, age, clothing palette and voice identity for every returning character. Never randomly redesign a character.
2. Photorealistic humans, clear eyes/hands/facial anatomy, clean and well-lit Telugu village home, kitchen and courtyard.
3. Every Telugu line must match the visible speaker's mouth movement. Only the active speaker talks; listeners show natural reactions.
4. Show motivated actions: turning, walking, cooking, serving, looking, pausing, smiling and responding. No frozen slideshow, generic loops or unrelated B-roll.
5. Maintain continuity of props, food, sarees, jewellery, room layout and screen direction.
6. Begin with a family disagreement; develop a meaningful story; include a natural like/share/subscribe request by a character; end with reconciliation and affection.
7. Target delivery: 9:16 vertical, approximately 2 minutes, natural Telugu dialogue and family-friendly tone.

## Mandatory quality gate
Do not publish until face consistency, distinct voices, Telugu pronunciation, speaker-specific lip-sync, natural movement, hands/faces and scene continuity pass review. If any fail, hold the video for correction.

The repository currently has a basic TTS and clip-concatenation pipeline. It does not yet implement verified multi-character talking-human video generation or Telugu lip-sync. Never describe a script, still image, generic clip or voice-over montage as a finished realistic AI episode.
